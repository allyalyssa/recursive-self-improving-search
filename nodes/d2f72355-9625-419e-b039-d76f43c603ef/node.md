### Inference and response
The intended skill is an entry node with relevant procedure children loaded as needed. Candidate methods, attempts and findings would retain their own bounded records under Commit rules. A successor must not silently overwrite the earlier method's grounds. Correct writing is not the search-performance scorer.

Jack noted that Pi permits deliberate context injection but questioned whether reminders could overcome idea basins. He left the choice between injection, self-directed retrieval and a learned feeder open to evidence. The assistant later called a purely mechanical feeder settled; that overstates Jack's decision. This node captures a design, not deployed instructions or a new global Commit protocol.
